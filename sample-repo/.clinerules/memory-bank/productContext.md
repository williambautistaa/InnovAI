# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists

Managing personal expenses is a common challenge. Many users need a fast, simple, and reliable way to log daily expenses and understand their monthly spending patterns. Existing solutions are often too complex, slow, or cluttered with features that distract from the core need: quick expense entry and clear summaries.

## Problems Solved

- **Frictionless Expense Logging:** Enables users to record expenses in seconds, reducing the barrier to consistent tracking.
- **Clear Monthly Insights:** Provides a straightforward summary of spending by category, helping users identify trends and make informed decisions.
- **No Unnecessary Complexity:** Focuses on essential features, avoiding the bloat of advanced analytics, multi-user support, or integrations that are not needed for an MVP.

## How It Should Work

- Users open the app and are greeted with a clean interface.
- Adding an expense is a one-step process: enter amount, category, and date, then save.
- The dashboard presents a monthly breakdown by category, including a simple chart for visual clarity.
- All actions are fast, with immediate feedback (e.g., toast notifications) and minimal loading times.

## User Experience Goals

- **Speed:** Every interaction should feel instant, from form submission to dashboard updates.
- **Simplicity:** The UI is uncluttered, with only the necessary fields and actions visible.
- **Reliability:** Data is saved and retrieved without errors, and the app works consistently across sessions.
- **Clarity:** Summaries and charts are easy to understand at a glance.

## MVP Focus

The product is intentionally limited to core features to ensure a polished, usable experience. Advanced features (e.g., receipt scanning, multi-user, export) are out of scope for the MVP.
